Risks & Limitations

6 items extracted
RISK · 01
Encrypted Data Incompatibility
The approach cannot be applied to encrypted data, as the entropy-based comparison will not work properly in such cases. The key of the encrypted drive must be extracted first to access an unencrypted image.
Page 56 · Section 5 — Discussion and Limitations
RISK · 02
Encrypted File System Limitations
Given the greater usage of encrypted file systems, the applicability of the proposed solution will be significantly limited in modern enterprise environments.
Page 56 · Section 5 — Discussion and Limitations
RISK · 03
Steganography Bypass
Relevant data stored in excluded file types using steganography techniques cannot be considered by the approach, creating a potential gap in evidence collection.
Page 56 · Section 5 — Discussion and Limitations
RISK · 04
Anti-Forensic Technique Interference
The filtering procedure will be interfered with in any case where anti-forensic techniques may have been used by a suspect, potentially compromising investigation integrity.
Page 56 · Section 5 — Discussion and Limitations
RISK · 05
Adversarial Manipulation
A clever attacker can learn the investigation procedure and modify files through specific manipulation, causing misidentification of relevant traces — described as one of the biggest limitations of the approach.
Page 56 · Section 5 — Discussion and Limitations
RISK · 06
Low Recall Performance
Without variance parameters, the algorithm achieved only 50% Recall, meaning too many irrelevant emails were not removed from the final investigation image, posing a privacy risk.
Page 55 · Section 4 — Evaluation, Table 4, Equation 7
