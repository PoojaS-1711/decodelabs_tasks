# Knowledge Analyst Dashboard — Task 3

## Overview
A document intelligence dashboard that simulates a 
RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) workflow by 
extracting key information from a research paper.

## Document Analyzed
"A Combined Approach for a Privacy-Aware Digital 
Forensic Investigation in Enterprises"
Ludwig Englbrecht & Günther Pernul (2021)

## What Was Extracted
- 6 Risks and Limitations
- 9 Key Dates
- 8 Stakeholders

## Citation Prompts Used
Prompts engineered to force the AI to cite specific 
page numbers and sections for every answer.
Screenshots of results included in this repository.
